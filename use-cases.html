SEVEN WAYS TO USE WREXLYN

One teammate.

Wrexlyn is one AI teammate you give plain-English instructions to. It plans the work, does it inside your own files and tools, checks its own result, and shows you the proof — across seven everyday jobs.

USE CASE 01 · SOFTWARE ENGINEERING

Ship working code, not just suggestions

“Fix a bug, add a feature, or clean up messy code — and prove it actually works.”

HOW IT WORKS
  1. 1Reads your actual codebase first, so changes fit how your project already works.
  2. 2Makes the change directly in your files — no copy-pasting code out of a chat window.
  3. 3Runs your real tests and build before calling the job done, and can undo everything with one click if something's wrong.
IN PLAIN WORDS

You ask: “Fix the login bug and make sure nothing else breaks.”

You get back a tested, working patch — with the test results to prove it.

📄

USE CASE 02 · BUSINESS DOCUMENTS

Get a finished file, not a wall of text

“Turn a request into a real Word report, Excel model, or PowerPoint deck.”

HOW IT WORKS
  1. 1Produces actual formatted documents — headings, tables, a working table of contents — not markdown pasted into a text box.
  2. 2Excel comes out with live, recalculating formulas, not numbers typed in by hand.
  3. 3PowerPoint slides use real, editable charts and layouts designed for the content, not just a title and five bullet points.
IN PLAIN WORDS

You ask: “Build a 10-slide investor deck from this research.”

You get an actual .pptx file, ready to open and present — today.

🔍

USE CASE 03 · RESEARCH & DILIGENCE

Answers you can actually check

“Answer a hard question, and show your work so I can trust it.”

HOW IT WORKS
  1. 1Pulls facts from your own files and the web, and keeps track of exactly where each one came from.
  2. 2Flags it when a new figure contradicts something it found earlier, instead of quietly overwriting it.
  3. 3Keeps the same numbers consistent across every report, memo, or slide it writes about the same topic.
IN PLAIN WORDS

You ask: “Summarize this company's financials and flag any red flags.”

You get a sourced write-up — every claim traceable back to evidence.

USE CASE 04 · SAFE EXPERIMENTATION

Let it take real action — without the fear

“I want it to actually do things, but I need an undo button I can trust.”

HOW IT WORKS
  1. 1Every file is captured exactly as it was, the instant before anything touches it.
  2. 2One click restores an entire turn's changes — no manual cleanup, no guessing what changed.
  3. 3A risky action always asks first; nothing destructive ever runs silently in the background.
IN PLAIN WORDS

You try a big refactor. It doesn't work out — one click, and every file is back exactly how it was.

🔌

USE CASE 05 · AUTOMATION

Put it to work inside your everyday tools

“Let it read and act inside the tools I already use — with my say-so at every step.”

HOW IT WORKS
  1. 1Connects to Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, OneDrive/SharePoint, Dropbox, and more.
  2. 2Anything it reads from outside is treated as information, never as a secret instruction to obey.
  3. 3You approve what it's allowed to touch — nothing acts on your accounts without permission.
IN PLAIN WORDS

You ask: “Check tomorrow's meetings and draft a follow-up email for each.”

It works inside your real calendar and inbox — you review before anything sends.

🧠

USE CASE 06 · MODEL FREEDOM

Pick the AI brain that fits the moment

“I don't want to be locked into one AI company — or pay for power I don't need.”

HOW IT WORKS
  1. 1Works with free and paid cloud models from multiple providers, side by side.
  2. 2Can run entirely on your own machine with a local model — nothing has to leave your computer.
  3. 3Same project, same memory, same rules — switch models mid-conversation without losing anything.
IN PLAIN WORDS

Use a free model for a quick task, switch to a stronger one for something hard — same conversation, same files.

USE CASE 07 · INDUSTRY SOLUTIONS

Purpose-built solutions for your industry

“I don't just want a general AI — I want something that already understands my industry.”

HOW IT WORKS
  1. 1The same underlying engine also powers ready-made, industry-specific products.
  2. 2Wrexlyn for Investments is the first: built for private equity teams doing screening, deal evaluation, and document review.
  3. 3It produces real IC notes and financial models — not generic chat answers about your deal. More industry-specific versions are on the way.

A PE analyst asks for an initial screen on a target company — and gets a structured evaluation with a real, editable financial model attached.
